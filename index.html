About

My research background spans recommender systems, data mining and algorithmic approaches to intelligent systems, with particular experience in sequential pattern mining and recommendation algorithms. I bring more than 15 years of industry experience, including leading teams developing machine learning models, AI solutions and intelligent applications.

My work combines algorithmic foundations with the design and development of applications and end-to-end systems. I am especially interested in the intersection of AI and Human-Computer Interaction (HCI), and in translating advances in intelligent systems into useful, understandable and responsive experiences.

Research areas

User feedback and feedback-driven AI

Understanding, modelling and learning from explicit and implicit feedback to improve intelligent systems over time.

User modelling and personalisation

Representing user preferences, behaviours and changing needs to support adaptive and personalised experiences.

Recommender systems and interactive AI

Developing recommendation approaches and interactive systems that incorporate user input and context.

Human-centred and explainable AI

Making AI systems more transparent, interpretable, interactive and responsive to human needs.

Topics of interest

I am interested in exploring these challenges across application domains where understanding individuals and their contexts matters, particularly healthcare and personalised services.
